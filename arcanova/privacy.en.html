3. Ads

Ads appear only when you tap “Watch an ad for 1 star”. Depending on your region, you’ll be asked for consent to personalized ads; if you decline, non-personalized ads are shown. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off personalized ads in your device settings.

4. Notifications

The “card of the day” reminder is scheduled on your device. No push token is sent to our server.

5. Your rights and deletion

In the App, go to Settings → Privacy → Delete my account and server data to immediately delete the account information stored on our server (stars, usage counts, recent results, photocards, membership status, linked email/Google sign-in). On-device history can be removed via Settings → Clear all history, or by deleting the App. Subscriptions must be cancelled separately in Google Play / App Store subscription settings; you can reconnect a membership with “Restore purchases” using the same store account. For other requests (access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection), contact us below.

6. EU/UK users (GDPR)

Legal bases: performance of a contract (providing readings, stars and membership), legitimate interests (security, abuse prevention, aggregated statistics) and consent (personalized ads, where required). You may withdraw consent at any time, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 14 (or the minimum age required in your country, such as 16 in some EU countries). If we learn that we processed a child’s information, we will delete it without delay.

8. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), server-only secret keys, minimal access rights, no storage or logging of original questions, hashed passwords and temporary lockout after failed sign-ins.
