Request deletion of my data

ARCANOVA : Idol Tarot

Your usage information is linked to the account created when you first open the App. If you linked email or Google sign-in, that sign-in information is deleted too.

1. Delete directly in the App

  1. Tap Settings in the bottom tab bar.
  2. Under Privacy, tap Delete my account and server data and confirm.

Stars, usage counts, recent reading results (up to 50), photocard collection, membership link, ad reward records and linked email/Google sign-in stored on our server are deleted immediately and cannot be restored. Original question text is never stored on our server in the first place. On-device history can be removed via Settings → Clear all history, or by deleting the App.

2. If you can’t use the App

Send “ARCANOVA data deletion request” to findtruthmind@gmail.com. To verify it’s you, please send it from the email you registered with, or we may need information from the App’s settings screen. We will delete your data within 7 days after verification.

3. Subscriptions

Deleting your data does not cancel your subscription automatically. Please cancel it in Google Play subscription settings.

4. Information we keep

Service statistics without account IDs or questions (counts and costs per model) are deleted automatically after 40 days. Store payment records are kept by Google as required by law.
